The problem

Exclusivity is a promise
nobody can check

$389.8B

Global retail sales of licensed merchandise and services, 2025. Licensing International, Global Licensing Industry Study 2026.

  • Almost every deal is carved up by territory × product category × time.
  • A licensee pays for "only you sell Mochi drinks in South Asia until 2028".
  • The licensor's book is confidential: competitors' names, territories and fees. So the licensee takes exclusivity on trust.
  • Overlapping grants surface after launch, as disputes.

Private, but nobody outside the agency can check them. Licensees just trust.

Anyone can verify, and every competitor gets your client list, territories and fees.

Midnight lets the licensor keep the book private and prove things about it.

Carve3
The product

Prove the book.
Never show it.

  • The licensor's book of grants lives on their device. The ledger holds only a commitment.
  • Issuing a grant comes with a zero-knowledge proof that it overlaps no exclusive grant in the whole book.
  • An overlap can't be issued: there is no proof for it, even from the licensor's own client.
  • The rights map shows the clash before proving. Try anyway, and the contract refuses.

Title, licensor key, book commitment, grant count, one commitment per grant, which are accepted, answers to checks
Private state
Identity secret · the licensor's book (16 grants + salt) · the licensee's grants
Off-chain
Each grant reaches its licensee as a private link, checked against its commitment

Carve6
Built on Midnight

Engineering

  • Compact 0.31.1 contract, 3 proven circuits: issue, accept, prove_rights.
  • Book commitment is Poseidon (transientHash): the issue key went from 76 MB to 19 MB. Grant commitments stay SHA-256, domain-separated and salted.
  • Time from a witness, bounded by blockTimeGte / blockTimeLt.
  • 28 simulator tests on the compiled circuits: overlaps, deleted or stale books, impostors, expiry, replayed checks. Mutation-checked.
  • React app on Lace DApp Connector v4, proving in the wallet. Live at carve-vert.vercel.app.
  • CLI on midnight-js 4.1.1 for scripted runs. Apache-2.0.

Market & adoption

Who it's for

First

Independent licensing agencies and character-IP studios with dozens to hundreds of grants per property, and the manufacturers who license from them.

Then

The same grid shape everywhere: music sync, franchise territories, sports media rights, distribution agreements.

Revenue

A per-book subscription for licensors, and a per-check fee for marketplaces and retailers that verify listings.

Adoption path: the licensee asks for a Carve link at signing. The agency needs only a browser and a wallet, with no data migration: the book starts with the next deal.
