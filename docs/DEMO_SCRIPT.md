# Carve: demo video script (about 3 minutes)

**Setup:** two Chrome profiles with Lace on Preprod, both funded:
- **Agency** (left): the licensor.
- **Chaiwala** (right): the licensee.

Do the deploy and one earlier grant before recording, so proving waits are short on camera.

## 0:00 The problem (20 s)
Over the home page:

> "If you license a character exclusively, say Mochi the Cat for drinks in South Asia, you're trusting the agency's spreadsheet that nobody else got the same rights. The agency can't show you their book, because it's full of their other clients. Carve fixes that on Midnight."

## 0:20 The licensee's ID (10 s)
1. **Right profile:** open **Licenses** and copy the licensee ID.
2. Say: *"This is all the agency needs from me."*

## 0:30 Issue a grant (50 s)
1. **Left profile:** open the Mochi book. The rights map shows the agency's private book. Point at **Started clean**.
2. Tap **S. Asia** and **Food**, choose 2 years, paste the ID, and type the terms "8% royalty".
3. Point at the green **Clear** verdict, then click **Issue grant** and approve in Lace.
4. While it proves, say: *"Lace is proving this grant overlaps nothing exclusive, against the whole book, without publishing any of it."*
5. The grant appears as #2. Copy the license link.

## 1:20 The contract refuses an overlap (30 s)
1. Tap **S. Asia**, **E. Asia** and **Food**. The S. Asia × Food cell turns red: *"Clashes with #2 (Chaiwala Foods)"*.
2. Paste another licensee ID and click **Try anyway**.
3. The contract refuses: *"Overlaps an exclusive grant already issued"*.
4. Say: *"Not our UI being careful. There is no proof for this grant, so it can't reach the chain."*

## 1:50 Accept (30 s)
1. **Right profile:** open the license link. The certificate shows four green checks:
   - issued, matching the commitment on the ledger;
   - names me;
   - the terms text matches;
   - accepted (pending).
2. Click **Accept license** and approve in Lace.

## 2:20 Prove to a retailer (35 s)
1. **Third window, no wallet needed with a Blockfrost token:** open the book. It shows *Private book* and only commitments.
2. Pick **South Asia** and **Food & drink**, then **Create check**. Copy the link.
3. **Right profile:** open the check link and click **Prove it**.
4. The retailer window flips to **EXCLUSIVE**: *"Proven with grant #2, in force on …"*.
5. Say: *"The retailer learns exactly one fact. Not the dates, not the royalty, not the other regions."*

## 2:55 Close (10 s)
> "Carve. Exclusive rights you can prove, from a book nobody can read. Built on Midnight."
